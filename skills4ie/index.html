Scope

Skills4IE’27 is the 1st International Workshop on Future Skills, Learning and Assessment for Intelligent Environments. It focuses on a timely question for the Intelligent Environments community: what should future professionals be able to do when artificial intelligence can already explain, generate, code, analyse and assist with increasingly sophisticated technical work?

Artificial intelligence, cyber-physical systems, IoT, edge computing and digital twins are changing both Intelligent Environments and the work of the people who design, build and operate them. At the same time, generative AI is changing how students learn, solve problems and demonstrate expertise.

Skills4IE’27 brings together researchers, educators and industry practitioners to discuss three closely connected questions:

  • Skills: What technical and transversal competencies will future Intelligent Environments professionals need?
  • Learning: How should computing and engineering education develop those competencies?
  • Assessment: How can we obtain credible evidence of competence when powerful AI assistance is readily available?

The workshop is particularly interested in systems reasoning, problem formulation, human–AI collaboration, critical evaluation, verification, transfer of knowledge, responsible decision-making and the relationship between professional practice and education.

Main topics

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

  • Future technical and transversal competencies for Intelligent Environments
  • Systems thinking, problem formulation and architectural reasoning
  • Human–AI collaboration in professional and educational practice
  • Critical evaluation and verification of AI-generated outputs
  • Responsible and trustworthy use of AI
  • Curriculum redesign in computing and engineering
  • Authentic, project-based, challenge-based and problem-based learning
  • Intelligent learning environments, simulations, laboratories and digital twins for learning
  • Assessment of reasoning, transfer, design judgement and decision-making
  • Assessment with and without AI
  • Oral, interactive, practical and process-based assessment
  • Academic integrity in the age of generative AI
  • Industry perspectives on changing professional roles and workforce needs
  • Programme-level and institutional educational transformation

Submissions

We welcome both mature research and shorter contributions that can stimulate discussion and help build a shared agenda for education and workforce development in Intelligent Environments.

Full papers

For mature research contributions, empirical studies, substantial educational designs, frameworks or evaluations.

Short papers

For emerging work, experience reports, industry perspectives, position papers and concise case studies.

Review and publication

Accepted papers intended for publication must receive at least two peer reviews. According to IE’27, accepted workshop papers will be published in the IOS Press Ambient Intelligence and Smart Environments series and made available electronically through the ACM Digital Library, subject to the final publication arrangements of the conference.

At least one author of each accepted paper must register for IE’27 at the standard registration rate and present the paper. The exact Skills4IE paper format, page limits and submission link will be announced once the workshop submission setup is finalised.

Workshop format

The workshop will combine paper presentations with structured discussion. Rather than treating presentations as isolated contributions, the programme will connect them around three questions: what should future professionals be able to do, how should those capabilities be developed, and what counts as convincing evidence that they have been developed?

IE’27 schedules workshops during the first two days of the conference. The exact Skills4IE’27 date and time will be announced when the conference programme is finalised.
